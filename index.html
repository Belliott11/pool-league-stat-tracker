Game Stats

Everyone in this game at a glance, including shooting splits and defensive/advanced numbers derived from the Shot Log below: FG/3PT/FT (made/attempted, %), eFG% (FG% weighted for 3s counting extra), TS% (true shooting — scoring efficiency across FGs and FTs combined), A/TO (assist-to-turnover ratio), Pts Allowed and Beaten (points scored, and times scored on, while they were the tagged defender on a make), Opp FG% (shooting % of everyone they were tagged defending, make or miss), Stops (times they were the tagged defender on a miss), GmSc (Game Score — a single "how good was this game" number), and Two-Way (GmSc plus Defensive Impact — Stops minus Beaten minus 0.4×Pts Allowed — so it's 0 for anyone never tagged as a defender, not a penalty for conservative tagging). STL/BLK are shown as recorded — their sum (Stocks) is in the CSV export if you want it, but isn't repeated here since it's just those two columns added together.

Season Rates (Individual)

Every counting stat here (PTS, OREB, DREB, AST, STL, BLK, TOV, PF, Pts Allowed, Beaten, Stops, Def Impact) is a rate per 20 combined points scored in the game, not a season total or a per-game average — only games with real shots logged count toward these at all, so a game that's just been rostered but not reviewed yet doesn't drag anyone's numbers down. Games are capped at different totals (16 or 21), so a plain per-game average isn't a fair comparison between a player who mostly plays 16-point games and one who mostly plays 21s — the game's combined final score stands in for "how much game happened," since possessions aren't tracked. Includes shooting splits and defensive/advanced numbers derived from each game's Shot Log — see the Game Stats table in Stat Entry for what each one means. Def Impact is Stops minus Beaten minus 0.4×Pts Allowed — 0 for anyone never tagged as a defender, not a penalty for conservative tagging. Two-Way is GmSc plus Def Impact. Click a player's name for their full game log and head-to-head history. Click a column header to sort by it (click again to flip direction).

League Shot Heatmap

Every marked field goal, league-wide, bucketed by court zone — color is FG% in that zone (red low, green high), the number is attempts. A zone stays blank until at least one shot lands in it. Only shots with a marked location are included (see Export → Backfill Shot Locations to fill in the rest).

Assist Connections

Every passer-to-scorer connection, league-wide — who set up whom, and how often. Directional: Alice assisting Bob is a separate row from Bob assisting Alice, since those are different facts. Sorted by assist count. Only connections with at least one assist are listed.

3PT Shot Distance

Every 3-pointer splits further by how far past the line it actually was — a shot just past it (Arc) is a normal, makeable three; a much longer near-pool-length heave (Deep) is a different, lower-percentage shot that the plain 3PT% on the table above blends in with it. Split by radial distance from the hoop against a threshold of 80 (in the shot chart's own 0-100 coordinates) — a single adjustable constant (`THREE_PT_DEEP_THRESHOLD` in app.js), drawn from an early, small sample, not a settled rule. Only 3-pointers with a marked shot location count (see Export → Backfill Shot Locations to fill in the rest), sorted by how many of those each player has. Treat any one player's split as an early read, not a verdict, until more games are logged.

Broken Session Video Links

A game can end up pointing at a session video id that no longer exists here — the video panel silently falls back to showing nothing useful, so this can go unnoticed for a while (an id can go stale if a browser profile changes, data gets imported from elsewhere, or a recording is removed some other way than the button above). A single-game export from a game listed here will carry a null masterVideo, even though masterVideoId is set.
